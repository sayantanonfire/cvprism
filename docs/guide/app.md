# The cvprism app (no terminal needed)

**Windows, nothing installed?** Download `cvprism-windows.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/sayantanonfire/cvprism/releases/latest), unzip it somewhere permanent (e.g. Documents), and double-click **Open cvprism**. Python is included.

Otherwise you don't need a terminal either. Double-click **`start.bat`** (Windows) or run **`start.sh`** (macOS/Linux). The first time, a setup page opens in your browser. After that, every double-click opens the cvprism app.

You can also open it any time with:

```bash
cvprism app
```

The app runs only on your own computer. It's protected by a one-time key, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose a sync transport.

## What you can do

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your evidence at a glance: how many cards, how many have public proof, your projects, your last CV |
| **Add evidence** | One achievement at a time: what you did, skills, how deep ("I used it" → "I published or taught it"), a result if you measured one, a proof link, the project. Tick "This isn't code" for papers, talks, field work, awards and teaching |
| **Bring in what you have** | Upload your LinkedIn data export (.zip) or an old CV (.docx, .md, .txt, .pdf) |
| **Projects** | Choose how each project appears: *Public* (blue proof links), *Private repository* (skills listed, no links) or *Local only* |
| **My evidence** | Your most recent cards and how strong their proof is |
| **Build a CV** | Pick a role, or paste a job posting. Download the PDF, Word and Markdown files, and read the ATS check, gap report and writing-quality report on the same page |

## Honesty is built in

The app uses the same engine as the command line:

- A number appears on your CV only if you typed it.
- Every line points to its evidence.
- Private work keeps its skills.
- Claude may polish wording, but it cannot add claims.

## On your other computers

Every computer can add evidence through the app. CVs are built on your main computer (the hub); other computers send their evidence there automatically.
